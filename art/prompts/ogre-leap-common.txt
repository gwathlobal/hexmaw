Pixel-art sprite sheet: a 3-frame LEAP attack of the OGRE, a hulking brute with a two-handed stone maul, for a Doom/Hexen-style first-person game where monsters are drawn as sprites from several camera angles.

LAYOUT
- One horizontal row of 4 equal cells, read left to right, each cell as wide as reference 2: cell 1 the standing copy, cells 2 to 4 the 3 frames.
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette, glow, cast shadow or floor.
- The cells are separated by thin vertical lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF), each running the full height of the image.
- Cell 1 is a plain copy of reference 2: the ogre standing, exactly as tall, in exactly the same pose. It sets the scale for the other frames, which are drawn at the same scale.
- The feet stand on the same ground line in every cell, at the same height as in reference 2. In the airborne frame the tucked feet rest on that line too: the game, not the drawing, lifts it.
- The whole figure, including the maul when it is raised overhead, fits inside each cell with a clear magenta margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped.
- No text, labels, numbers, frame borders, speed lines, motion blur, smear trails, sparks, dust, debris, cracks in the floor, blood or impact effects. Only the ogre and its maul.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no pink or purple glow bleeding into the background.

THE LEAP: the ogre crouches, springs high through the air toward the player, and lands maul-first in a shockwave. Both hands grip the haft in frames 3 and 4, the right hand nearer the stone head.

- Frame 4 (cell 4), LANDING: crashing down, the feet planted wide and the knees deeply bent from the impact, the body bent far forward, the maul driven straight down into the floor in front of the feet in both hands, the stone head at floor level, the arms straight.
