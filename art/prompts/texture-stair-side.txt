THE SURFACE: the risers and side faces of a dungeon STAIRCASE, built of small dressed stone blocks.
- Eight courses of stone from top to bottom, each course the same height, so the pattern repeats exactly vertically. Blocks within a course are one and a half to three times as long as they are tall, neatly squared, joints staggered between courses like brickwork.
- Cool slate-grey stone with a faint blue cast, each block a slightly different shade, faces smooth and worn, with a lighter worn top edge on every block, a few chipped corners and hairline cracks.
- Thin dark joints. Cleaner and more regular than a rough dungeon wall, a little lighter than it.
