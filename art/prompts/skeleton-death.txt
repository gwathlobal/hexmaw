Pixel-art sprite sheet: the 8-frame death animation of the SKELETON, a dead warrior raised by a necromancer, for a Doom/Hexen-style first-person game where monsters are drawn as sprites. The death is seen from the front only. The last frame is the corpse, a pile of bones, which stays on the floor. (The game also plays these frames backwards, as the skeleton reassembling itself out of the pile and standing up, so each frame must follow smoothly from the one before.)

REFERENCE IMAGES, in the order attached:
1. The skeleton's turnaround sheet. It is the character design: a bare human skeleton of a tall man standing hunched, knees a little bent; old yellowed bone stained brown in the joints and cracks; a grinning skull with two small glowing sickly green eyes deep in the sockets; a rotted dark leather belt at the hips with a torn strip of dark cloth hanging from it; a dented, rusted iron guard on the LEFT forearm; and in its RIGHT hand an old SWORD with a notched, rusty blade. No flesh, skin or organs anywhere. Also the shading style.
2. The skeleton standing, seen from the front. Match its pixel size, outline weight and scale exactly: frame 1 is exactly as tall as reference 2.

THE DEATH: whatever holds the bones together lets go, and it collapses where it stands.
- Frame 1, HIT: standing as in reference 2 but jolted back, the skull knocked back, the jaw open, the eyes flaring.
- Frame 2, LOOSENING: the bones start to come apart at the joints, small gaps opening at the neck, shoulders, elbows and hips; the sword slipping from the hand.
- Frame 3, BUCKLING: the legs give way at the knees, the whole body dropping straight down, coming apart, the skull tipping, the sword falling.
- Frame 4, COLLAPSING: the rib cage and skull dropping onto the pelvis and leg bones, which splay apart on the floor, half the standing height.
- Frame 5, SCATTERING: the bones crash onto the floor in a heap, a few long bones sliding out, the skull falling onto the heap.
- Frame 6, SETTLING: a low heap of bones, the rib cage on its side, the skull rolling to rest on top, the eyes dimming.
- Frame 7, STILL: the heap at rest, the skull on top facing the viewer, the eyes almost dark, the sword lying on the floor beside the heap.
- Frame 8, CORPSE: the same low pile of bones with the skull on top and the sword beside it. The eyes dark: no glow anywhere. This frame stays on screen.

LAYOUT
- A grid of 2 rows of 4 equal cells: frames 1 to 4 in the top row, 5 to 8 in the bottom row, each read left to right.
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette, glow, cast shadow or floor.
- The cells are separated by thin lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF): vertical lines between the columns, each running the full height of the image, and one horizontal line between the rows, running the full width.
- Each frame follows on smoothly from the one before: it falls in one place, without jumps between frames, and once down it never leaves the floor.
- The same scale in every frame. In every frame the figure or what is left of it rests on the same ground line near the bottom of its cell; nothing reaches below it, and nothing floats.
- Everything fits inside each cell with a clear magenta margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped.
- No text, labels, numbers, frame borders, speed lines, motion blur, dust, sparks, blood pools or gore.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no pink or purple glow bleeding into the background.
