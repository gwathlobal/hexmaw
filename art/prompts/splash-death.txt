
THE SCENE: THE DEATH SCREEN, shown when the player has died
- The aftermath, on the floor of a dark stone dungeon hall, seen from low down, as if from the flagstones. Rough grey stone blocks, a thick pillar on each side, the hall behind fading into black fog.
- The calm dark area is bigger than usual here: the middle third of the picture's width must stay deep shadow and black fog over nearly its whole height, since the game writes a title, several lines of text and a button there. No faces, no bright light and no important detail in it. Only dark flagstones at its very bottom.
- At the LOWER LEFT, the dead delver: an armoured body slumped against the left pillar, seen from the side, head fallen forward and hidden by the shadow, no visible face. One steel gauntlet lies open on the floor, palm up, fingers slack, in the design of the sword reference. Beside it the steel-rimmed round-topped shield from the shield reference lies on its side, dented, and the sword from the sword reference has fallen flat on the stones, out of reach.
- At the LOWER RIGHT, the red IMP from the reference, crouched on the flagstones, claws wet, grinning sideways at its kill. It is whole in the picture and does not cross into the calm centre.
- On the right pillar above the imp, an iron wall torch from the torch reference, guttering low: a small dying orange flame, throwing a little warm light on the imp and the nearest stones.
- A dark pool of blood spreads across the flagstones from the delver toward the bottom of the picture, with a few smears and spatters. A dim ember-red glow low on the floor around it. Nothing bright.
- The mood is still and final: the fight is over, and no one is coming.
