Pixel-art sprite sheet: the 8-frame death animation of the ARC VOTARY, a blind monk who holds a beam of lightning between its hands, for a Doom/Hexen-style first-person game where monsters are drawn as sprites. The death is seen from the front only. The last frame is the corpse, which stays on the floor.

REFERENCE IMAGES, in the order attached:
1. The votary's turnaround sheet. It is the character design: a tall, gaunt humanoid of a man's height, as tall as the acolyte, standing upright and still, slightly stooped from the shoulders; a shaven head, bare and pale, its EYES SEWN SHUT, a row of dark stitches across each closed lid; a long ankle-length monk's HABIT of undyed pale linen gone grey-brown with grime, with COPPER WIRE stitched through it in bright lines and spirals down the chest, sleeves and hem; a heavy dark LODESTONE, a rough lump of black-grey iron stone, hanging on two iron chains at the middle of its chest; copper bands round both wrists; bare feet under the hem; its two HANDS held apart in front of the chest, palms facing each other a forearm's length apart, with a few pale yellow-white SPARKS jumping between them. Also the shading style.
2. The votary standing, seen from the front. Match its pixel size, outline weight and scale exactly: frame 1 is exactly as tall as reference 2.

THE DEATH: the lightning it held turns on it, and it falls.
- Frame 1, HIT: standing as in reference 2 but jolted back, the head snapped up, the sparks between its hands bursting outward.
- Frame 2, BURN-OUT: pale yellow-white lightning crawling over its whole body along the copper wire, the arms flung wide and rigid, the lodestone split by a bright crack.
- Frame 3, BUCKLING: the lightning gone, smoke-dark scorch along the wire, it drops to its knees, the arms falling.
- Frame 4, TOPPLING: kneeling, it pitches forward, the head bowed, the lodestone swinging on its chains.
- Frame 5, FALLING: its body falling forward toward the floor.
- Frame 6, DOWN: lying face down on the floor, the habit spread, the arms out to the sides.
- Frame 7, STILL: lying face down, the split lodestone beside it on its chains, a last tiny spark on one copper band.
- Frame 8, CORPSE: the same, lying still, every spark gone, the copper dulled. This frame stays on screen.

LAYOUT
- A grid of 2 rows of 4 equal cells: frames 1 to 4 in the top row, 5 to 8 in the bottom row, each read left to right.
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette, glow, cast shadow or floor.
- The cells are separated by thin lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF): vertical lines between the columns, each running the full height of the image, and one horizontal line between the rows, running the full width.
- Each frame follows on smoothly from the one before: it falls in one place, without jumps between frames, and once down it never leaves the floor.
- The same scale in every frame. In every frame the figure rests on the same ground line near the bottom of its cell; nothing reaches below it, and nothing floats.
- Everything fits inside each cell with a clear magenta margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped.
- No text, labels, numbers, frame borders, speed lines, motion blur, dust, blood pools or gore.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no pink, purple or cyan glow bleeding into the background.
