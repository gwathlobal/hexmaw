
VIEW: directly from the front, as in the first cell of reference 1 and in reference 2. The imp faces the viewer squarely in both frames; both eyes, both arms and both horns are visible.
- Frame 1: the backward bend shows as the head tipped back so we look up under the chin and see the open shrieking mouth from below, the shoulders hiked up, both arms thrown wide to image-left and image-right with claws splayed, the knees bent and pointing out. The tail tip whips up beside one hip.
- Frame 2: we look down on the top of the bowed head and horns; the face is tilted down but the slitted eyes and gritted fangs still show. The imp's right hand (on image-left) clutches the middle of its chest; its left arm (on image-right) hangs out from the body. The body is lower than standing, knees bent.
