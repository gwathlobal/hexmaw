Pixel-art sprite sheet: the BLOODROOT'S BLOOD SEED, a projectile for a Doom/Hexen-style first-person dungeon game: a seed the monstrous plant spits, which bursts on the floor into roots.

REFERENCE IMAGES, in the order attached:
1. The bloodroot, the plant that spits it: match its colours, knotted root bark, wet crimson flesh, bone thorns and glowing veins.
2. The house style anchor: pixel size, lighting and palette.

THE FRAMES:
- Top row, cells 1 to 4, FLIGHT, a looping tumble: a round seed pod the size of a fist, dark red with a crust of root bark, glowing red veins, two or three small thorns and a short root tail, turning a quarter turn each frame, a few drops of blood around it.
- Bottom row, cells 5 to 9, BURST on hitting the floor: cell 5 the pod cracking open with a splash of dark blood; cell 6 a wet red burst, thin root tendrils shooting out sideways along the ground; cell 7 the tendrils spread wider and curling, the blood spattering; cell 8 the tendrils settling flat, the splash falling; cell 9 a few last drops and fading tendril tips.
- The pod is about a sixth of the cell's height; the burst fills most of its cell, wider than tall, standing on the bottom of the cell.
- LAYOUT: two rows of five equal cells: the flight in cells 1 to 4 of the top row, its fifth cell empty; the burst in all five cells of the bottom row. Background flat pure magenta (#FF00FF); cells separated by thin lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF). Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line.
- No text, labels, frame borders, speed lines or motion blur. Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no pink or purple glow bleeding into the background.
