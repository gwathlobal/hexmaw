Pixel-art sprite sheet: the pain (flinch) animation of the RIFTWALKER, an assassin who steps through rifts in the air, pulls its victims to it and stabs them, for a Doom/Hexen-style first-person game where monsters are drawn as sprites from several camera angles.

REFERENCE IMAGES, in the order attached:
1. The riftwalker's turnaround sheet (front, front three-quarter, side, rear three-quarter, back). It is the character design: a lean, wiry humanoid in a low knife-fighter's crouch, knees bent, weight forward; a close hood and short cowl in charcoal, and under the hood a smooth featureless oval MASK of polished brass with no eyes, mouth or nose; tight wrappings of charcoal and deep indigo round arms, chest and legs, a short split tabard to mid-thigh, wrapped boots; thin cracks of pale silver-blue light along the seams of the wrappings; a long CURVED DAGGER of pale steel in its right hand, held low; a small hard-edged diamond SHARD of pale silver-blue light floating over its open left palm. Also the shading style.
2. The riftwalker standing, seen from THIS sheet's camera view. Match its camera angle, the way it faces, its pixel size, outline weight, scale and ground line exactly.

It has two arms and two legs, no more. The dagger stays in its right hand in every frame. The mask stays smooth and featureless. Any rift light is pale silver-blue, drawn as solid pixels on or right next to the figure, never a soft halo on the magenta.

LAYOUT
- One horizontal row of 3 equal cells, read left to right, each cell as wide as reference 2: cell 1 the standing copy, cells 2 to 3 the 2 frames.
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette, glow, cast shadow or floor.
- The cells are separated by thin vertical lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF), each running the full height of the image.
- Cell 1 is a plain copy of reference 2: the riftwalker standing, exactly as tall, in exactly the same pose. It sets the scale for the other frames, which are drawn at the same scale.
- The feet stay on the same ground line in every cell, at the same height as in reference 2.
- The whole figure, including the dagger, fits inside each cell with a clear magenta margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped.
- No text, labels, numbers, frame borders, speed lines, motion blur, smear trails, sparks, dust or light rays. Only the riftwalker.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no pink, purple or cyan glow bleeding into the background.

THE FLINCH: a sharp, readable reaction to a blow, in the spirit of a Doom pain frame. It plays for a third of a second when it is struck by a sword or a fire bolt. It must read clearly as hurt, not as an attack.
- Cell 2, JOLT: the instant of the hit. The head snapped back, the mask tipped up, the chest thrust back, both arms flung out low, the dagger hand open but still gripping, the shard flickering out to a point; the knees buckling.
- Cell 3, STAGGER: a moment later. It doubles forward, lower than standing, the left arm clutching its middle, the dagger held loosely down, the head bowed, the cracks of light dimmed.
