
THIS PANEL: a tall LANCET window of the Ashen Temple, a priesthood of fire and ash, exactly 2.5 times as tall as it is wide.
- The top ends in a pointed gothic arch. The two top corners of the rectangle outside the arch are filled with SOLID PURE BLACK, as part of the lead.
- The design: a bronze CENSER URN near the bottom, and rising out of it a column of curling SMOKE in pale lilac and dusty rose that climbs the window, with glowing embers of orange and gold drifting up through it; in the arch's point, a small black-rimmed SUN OF CINDERS, an ember-orange disc with short flame rays.
- The background glass is deep wine red and dark burgundy cut into small irregular pieces.
- A narrow border band of alternating small amber and orange pieces runs up both sides inside the outer lead.
