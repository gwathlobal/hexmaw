Pixel-art sprite sheet: the 8-frame death animation of the RIME HAG, a witch of the frozen caves, for a Doom/Hexen-style first-person game where monsters are drawn as sprites. The death is seen from the front only. The last frame is the corpse, which stays on the floor.

REFERENCE IMAGES, in the order attached:
1. The rime hag's turnaround sheet. It is the character design: a stooped, hunched old crone of a little under a man's height, bent at the back, leaning her weight on a staff in her left hand; a long cloak and hood of shaggy GREY FUR stiff and frosted white with hoarfrost at its edges; a MANTLE OF ICICLES hanging from her shoulders over the fur like a short cape of pale blue-white spikes; long thin arms and bony hands with BLUE-WHITE SKIN, a gaunt old face under the hood with pale white eyes, long lank white hair; a STAFF OF BLACK ICE, dark and glassy, taller than she is, topped by a FROZEN LANTERN, a small iron cage around a cold glowing core of pale cyan-white light; ragged hems, bare frostbitten feet. Also the shading style.
2. The rime hag standing, seen from the front. Match her pixel size, outline weight and scale exactly: frame 1 is exactly as tall as reference 2.
3. The game's style anchor sheet, for the style only.

THE DEATH: the cold that held her together lets go. Her icicle mantle cracks and she sinks into a heap of fur and rime.
- Frame 1, HIT: standing as in reference 2 but jolted back, the hood thrown back, the lantern's light flickering.
- Frame 2, CRACK: jagged cracks run through the icicle mantle and up her blue-white arms; her mouth open; the staff slipping in her grip.
- Frame 3, SHATTER: the icicle mantle breaks, pieces of pale ice falling round her as solid pixels; she sags at the knees, the staff toppling away from her.
- Frame 4, SINKING: on her knees, bent forward, the fur cloak spreading round her, the staff lying on the floor beside her, the lantern dark.
- Frame 5, SLUMPING: she folds forward into the fur, her head down, one bony hand on the floor.
- Frame 6, HEAP: no more than a low heap of grey fur, frost and broken icicles on the floor, one hand showing.
- Frame 7, SETTLING: the heap settles lower, white frost spreading over it.
- Frame 8, CORPSE: a low heap of frosted grey fur and broken icicles, the black ice staff lying across the floor in front of it, its lantern dark. This frame stays on screen.

LAYOUT
- A grid of 2 rows of 4 equal cells: frames 1 to 4 in the top row, 5 to 8 in the bottom row, each read left to right.
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette, glow, cast shadow or floor.
- The cells are separated by thin lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF): vertical lines between the columns, each running the full height of the image, and one horizontal line between the rows, running the full width.
- Each frame follows on smoothly from the one before: she falls in one place, without jumps between frames, and once down never leaves the floor.
- The same scale in every frame. In every frame the figure rests on the same ground line near the bottom of its cell; nothing reaches below it, and nothing floats.
- Everything fits inside each cell with a clear magenta margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped.
- No text, labels, numbers, frame borders, speed lines, motion blur, mist, snow, blood pools or gore.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no pink, purple or cyan glow bleeding into the background.

HOUSE STYLE (Hexmaw; attach art/style/anchor.png as a style reference, and say which reference it is):
- Crisp pixel art at the same pixel size as the reference sheet: about 64 pixels per metre for anything in the world, every pixel square and hard-edged, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no soft glow, no semi-transparent pixels, no dithering gradients.
- Lit from the upper left: lighter top-left faces, darker bottom-right ones, a few hard-edged tones per material, the same contrast as the imp and the knight on the reference.
- Colours from the reference's palette: warm grey-brown stone, dark iron, muted earth tones, and bright saturated colour only for fire, magic and blood.
- Outlines as on the reference: the shape's edge a dark tone of what it encloses, not a uniform black line.
