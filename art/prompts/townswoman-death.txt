Pixel-art sprite sheet: the 8-frame death animation of the TOWNSWOMAN, a woman of a grim walled settlement deep underground, for a Doom/Hexen-style first-person game where characters are drawn as sprites. The death is seen from the front only. The last frame is the corpse, which stays on the floor.

REFERENCE IMAGES, in the order attached:
1. The townswoman's turnaround sheet (front, front three-quarter, side, rear three-quarter, back). It is the character design: a gaunt, careworn woman of average height with a hard, tired face; a ragged grey wool SHAWL over her head and shoulders like a hood, its fringed ends hanging over her chest; a long, faded dark-red dress to the ankles, patched, with a narrow brown belt; worn brown shoes; a wicker BASKET held in front of her in both hands, at the waist. Also the shading style.
2. The townswoman standing, seen from the front. Match its pixel size, outline weight and scale exactly: frame 1 is exactly as tall as reference 2.

THE DEATH: she is struck down and falls where she stands.
- Frame 1, HIT: standing as in reference 2 but jolted back, the head knocked back, the basket jerking.

- Frame 8, CORPSE: the same, still. This frame stays on screen.

LAYOUT
- A grid of 2 rows of 4 equal cells: frames 1 to 4 in the top row, 5 to 8 in the bottom row, each read left to right.
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette, glow, cast shadow or floor.
- The cells are separated by thin lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF): vertical lines between the columns, each running the full height of the image, and one horizontal line between the rows, running the full width.
- Each frame follows on smoothly from the one before: it falls in one place, without jumps between frames, and once down it never leaves the floor.
- The same scale in every frame. In every frame the figure rests on the same ground line near the bottom of its cell; nothing reaches below it, and nothing floats.
- Everything fits inside each cell with a clear magenta margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped.
- No text, labels, numbers, frame borders, speed lines, motion blur, dust, sparks, blood pools or gore.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no pink or purple glow bleeding into the background.
