
VIEW: front three-quarter, as in the second cell of reference 1 and in reference 2. The imp faces toward IMAGE-LEFT and slightly toward the viewer in both frames; we see its face, chest and its left side.

- Frame 2: the imp doubles over toward image-left, head ducked, one hand clutching its chest, the far arm hanging loose. Knees deeply bent, the back foot shuffled toward image-right. The tail droops low on image-right.
