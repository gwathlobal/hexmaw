Pixel-art first-person weapon view for a Doom/Hexen-style dungeon game: the player's own two armoured hands, seen from the player's eyes, from behind, as in Hexen or Heretic, where the weapon sprite sits at the bottom of the screen. One single frame, not a sprite sheet.

REFERENCE IMAGE 1 is the game's existing first-person SWORD frame: a right gauntlet (steel plates, brass rivets, a leather-and-brass cuff, a steel vambrace running off the bottom-right corner) holding an arming sword upright with a brass crossguard. It sets the style: the pixel size, the dark outline weight, the shading, the colours, and the scale of the hand.

THE PICTURE
- A wide 16:9 image (1536 x 864 drawn in the middle of the 1536 x 1024 canvas is fine; the rest magenta).
- The RIGHT hand and sword sit in the bottom-right corner exactly as in reference 1: same pose, same size, same place, the forearm running off the bottom-right edge.
- The LEFT hand holds a SHIELD in the bottom-left corner. The left gauntlet is the mirror twin of the right one (same plates, rivets, cuff and vambrace), its forearm running off the bottom edge near the left corner. We see the shield from behind and a little to its side, as the player holding it would: mostly its BACK (the inner face with its leather straps and the gauntlet's grip on them) and its rim, turned partly toward the middle of the screen so a strip of its front face and rim shows too. It is held low and relaxed, ready, not raised: its top edge stands at about the lower third of the screen and its bottom and the arm are cut off by the bottom edge.
- The middle of the screen stays empty: it is where the player looks.
- The camera is the player's eyes: the hands are the nearest things, much bigger than they would be in a third-person view. No body, no shoulders, no head, no legs.

STRICT RULES
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere around the hands, shield and sword: no floor, no walls, no gradient, no vignette, no glow, no shadow.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels and no pink or purple glow bleeding into the background.
- No text, labels, frame borders, grid lines, motion blur or effects. Only the two hands, the sword and the shield.
