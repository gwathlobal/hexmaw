
THE SHIELD: a heater shield of BLACKENED STEEL (dark iron, almost black, with cold grey highlights) with a raised BRASS RIM and a row of brass rivets along it, the same brass as the sword's crossguard and the gauntlet's rivets. Its inner face is dark worn leather over wood with two thick leather straps on brass buckles; the left gauntlet grips the upper strap. The steel has a few dents and scratches. Its top corner nearest the screen's middle rises a little above the rest.
