Pixel-art character turnaround sheet for a monster in a Doom/Hexen-style first-person dark-fantasy dungeon game, where monsters are drawn as sprites seen from several camera angles: the GALE WRAITH, a dead storm-caller that floats just off the floor, keeps its distance and summons tornadoes.

REFERENCE IMAGES, in the order attached: turnaround sheets of four other monsters from the same game (a red imp, a hooded robed acolyte, an armoured knight, and a gaunt necromancer with a staff). Use them ONLY for the art style and the sheet layout: pixel size, outline weight, shading, how the views are arranged and how far each one is turned. Do not copy anything of their designs. In particular the wraith must NOT look like the acolyte: no solid robe, no orange or oxblood, no fire; and not like the necromancer: no staff, no beard, no bare head, no green.

THE GALE WRAITH
- A thin, tall, floating figure, about as tall as the acolyte, with NO LEGS AND NO FEET. Below the waist the body is only long hanging strips of rag that taper to points, ending at the ground line.
- A deep, ragged HOOD with its point blown back, and in the black inside it NO FACE, only two small, level GLOWING EYES, pale storm violet.
- It is made of RAGS: long torn strips of cloth in storm grey, slate blue and faded dusty violet, wound loosely round a body that can hardly be seen. Dark gaps between the strips show nothing behind them. The strips are all swept the same way, as if in a wind, streaming out to one side of the figure, which gives it a jagged, unmistakable silhouette.
- Long, thin arms held out a little from the body, wrapped in grey bandages trailing loose ends, the hands bare, ash grey, with long bony fingers, spread and slightly raised.
- A cold, washed-out palette, unlike the imp's red, the acolyte's oxblood and fire, the knight's steel or the necromancer's black and green: storm grey, slate blue, dusty violet, ash, and bright pale violet only for the glowing eyes.

LAYOUT
- One horizontal row of 5 equal cells, read left to right: FRONT (facing the viewer), FRONT THREE-QUARTER, SIDE (profile), REAR THREE-QUARTER, BACK (facing away).
- In the front three-quarter, side and rear three-quarter views the wraith faces IMAGE-LEFT.
- The same figure at the same scale in every cell, its lowest rag tips on the same ground line, in the same pose; only the camera angle changes.
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette, glow, cast shadow or floor.
- The cells are separated by thin vertical lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF), each running the full height of the image.
- The whole figure, including every streaming rag, fits inside each cell with a clear magenta margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped.
- No text, labels, numbers, frame borders, smoke, sparks, dust or wind lines outside the figure. The violet glow stays in the eyes, never as a halo on the magenta.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no pink or purple glow bleeding into the background.
