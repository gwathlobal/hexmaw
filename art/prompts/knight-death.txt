Pixel-art sprite sheet: the 8-frame death animation of the KNIGHT, a slow, heavily armoured warrior behind a huge rune-carved tower shield, for a Doom/Hexen-style first-person game where monsters are drawn as sprites. The death is seen from the front only. The last frame is the corpse, which stays on the floor.

LAYOUT

- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette, glow, cast shadow or floor.
- The cells are separated by thin lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF): vertical lines between the columns, each running the full height of the image, and one horizontal line between the rows, running the full width.
- Each frame follows on smoothly from the one before: the knight falls in one place, without jumps between frames, and from frame 4 on it never leaves the floor.
- The same scale in every frame. In every frame the knight or the heap rests on the same ground line near the bottom of its cell; nothing reaches below it, and nothing floats.
- The whole figure, the shield and the mace fit inside each cell with a clear magenta margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped.
- No text, labels, numbers, frame borders, speed lines, motion blur, dust, sparks, blood or gore.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no pink or purple glow bleeding into the background.
