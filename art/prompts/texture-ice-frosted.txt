THIS VARIANT: old, FROSTED, scuffed ice, milky and opaque.
- Pale grey-blue and steel blue, cloudy and matt, with feathery white frost patterns in drifts and patches.
- Many crossing skid scratches and gouges, some short hairline cracks, and a few darker smoother patches where the frost has been worn off.
- Muted and grimy rather than bright: an ice floor that has been walked on for years.
