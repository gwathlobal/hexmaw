
THIS PANEL: a tall LANCET window of the Deepwardens, the keepers of the deep ways under the earth, exactly 2.5 times as tall as it is wide.
- The top ends in a pointed gothic arch. The two top corners of the rectangle outside the arch are filled with SOLID PURE BLACK, as part of the lead.
- The design: a great iron LANTERN held up on a tall staff in the upper half, its glass glowing warm gold, and below it a STAIRWAY of stone steps descending into the deep, the steps in slate blue and teal, each lower step darker; crossed behind the staff, a large old KEY in pale steel blue.
- The background glass is deep cobalt and midnight blue cut into small irregular pieces, with a few pieces of dark teal.
- A narrow border band of alternating small teal and gold pieces runs up both sides inside the outer lead.
