Pixel-art sprite sheet: the WALK cycle of the GUARD, a guard of the town watch in a grim walled settlement deep underground, for a Doom/Hexen-style first-person game where characters are drawn as sprites from several camera angles.

REFERENCE IMAGES, in the order attached:
1. The guard's turnaround sheet (front, front three-quarter, side, rear three-quarter, back). It is the character design: an ordinary weathered man of average height with a short dark beard; a dented, open-faced round steel helmet with a brim; a mail shirt to the thighs with mail sleeves; over it a plain sleeveless tabard of BRIGHT PURE GREEN (#00C000) with no emblem, ragged at its hem; a brown leather belt with a pouch; brown trousers and leather boots; a tall SPEAR in his RIGHT hand and a round wooden SHIELD with an iron rim and boss on his LEFT arm. Also the shading style.
2. The guard standing, seen from THIS sheet's camera view. Match its camera angle, the way it faces, its pixel size, outline weight, scale and ground line exactly.

The spear stays in the right hand and the shield on the left arm in every frame. Never swap them, never draw a second spear or shield. The tabard stays the same flat bright green (#00C000) in every frame: it is recoloured in the game.

LAYOUT
- One horizontal row of 5 equal cells, read left to right, each cell as wide as reference 2: cell 1 the standing copy, cells 2 to 5 the 4 frames.
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette, glow, cast shadow or floor.
- The cells are separated by thin vertical lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF), each running the full height of the image.
- Cell 1 is a plain copy of reference 2: the figure standing, exactly as tall, in exactly the same pose. It sets the scale for the other frames, which are drawn at the same scale.
- The feet stand on the same ground line in every cell, at the same height as in reference 2. It does not hop or float.
- The whole figure fits inside each cell with a clear magenta margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped.
- No text, labels, numbers, frame borders, speed lines, motion blur, smear trails, sparks, dust, blood or impact effects. Only the figure.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no pink or purple glow bleeding into the background.

THE WALK: a steady, watchful patrol walk, not hurried: the back straight, the spear carried upright in the right hand, its butt just off the ground and its head bobbing a little with the steps, the shield held at his left side. The body rises a little on the passing frames and sinks on the contact frames.
- Frame 2 (cell 2), LEFT CONTACT: the left foot forward and planted, the right foot back, the legs at their widest.
- Frame 3 (cell 3), PASSING: the right leg swinging forward past the planted left one, knee bent, foot just off the ground; the body at its highest.
- Frame 4 (cell 4), RIGHT CONTACT: the right foot forward and planted, the left foot back, the legs at their widest.
- Frame 5 (cell 5), PASSING: the left leg swinging forward past the planted right one; the body at its highest.
The four frames loop: after frame 5 comes frame 2 again.
