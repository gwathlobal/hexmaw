Pixel-art sprite sheet: the HARPY KNOCKED OUT OF THE AIR, for a Doom/Hexen-style first-person game where monsters are drawn as sprites. The harpy is a winged hag that dives at the player; a blow during its dive knocks it down onto the floor, where it lies stunned for a moment before it struggles up and takes off again. Seen from the front only.

REFERENCE IMAGES, in the order attached:
1. The harpy's turnaround sheet. It is the character design: a gaunt hag with grey leathery skin, a hooked, beak-like nose, a shrieking mouth of small yellowed teeth, glowing sickly yellow eyes and long matted ash-grey hair; a body of ragged slate and soot-brown feathers; ARMS THAT ARE WINGS, with three long, bony, rust-clawed fingers at each wing's bend; scaly dark bird legs with big hooked rust-coloured talons; a short fan of tail feathers. Also the shading style.
2. The harpy hovering, seen from the front. Match its pixel size, outline weight and scale exactly: cell 1 is a plain copy of it.

The harpy has exactly two wings and two legs in every frame, and no human arms or hands besides the clawed fingers at the wings' bends. It is alive and unhurt by the fall: no blood, no broken limbs.

- The whole figure, both wings and the talons, fits inside each cell with a clear magenta margin, even the wings spread flat on the floor. Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped.

- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no pink or purple glow bleeding into the background.
