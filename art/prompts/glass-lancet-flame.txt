
THIS PANEL: a tall LANCET window, exactly 2.5 times as tall as it is wide.
- The top ends in a pointed gothic arch. The two top corners of the rectangle outside the arch are filled with SOLID PURE BLACK, as part of the lead.
- The design: a single tall stylised FLAME rising from a small brazier bowl near the bottom, its tongues in ruby red, orange and amber gold, licking up into the arch's point, where it ends in a small six-pointed spark.
- Behind the flame, the background glass is a deep blue-violet night, cut into small irregular pieces.
- A narrow border band of alternating small red and gold pieces runs up both sides inside the outer lead.
