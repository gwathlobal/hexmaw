Pixel-art character turnaround sheet for a monster in a Doom/Hexen-style first-person dark-fantasy dungeon game, where monsters are drawn as sprites seen from several camera angles: the HARPY, a winged hag that circles under the dungeon's ceiling and dives at the player's head with its talons.

REFERENCE IMAGES, in the order attached: turnaround sheets of three other monsters from the same game (a red imp, a robed acolyte, and an armoured knight). Use them ONLY for the art style and the sheet layout: pixel size, outline weight, shading, how the views are arranged and how far each one is turned. Do not copy anything of their designs.

THE HARPY
- A gaunt, bird-like hag, its body about as tall as the imp's from the crown of its head to the tips of its hanging talons, but much wider with its wings. It is HOVERING in the air, not standing: the body upright, the legs hanging below it with the talons half-open, ready to grab.
- Its ARMS ARE ITS WINGS: two large wings of ragged, dark feathers growing from its shoulders, with three long, bony, clawed fingers at each wing's bend. Both wings are RAISED UP AND BACK in a high V above its shoulders, mid-beat, so their tips rise above its head. They are not spread out flat to the sides.
- A thin, wiry upper body covered in rough grey-brown feathers from the throat down, a shaggy mantle of longer feathers over the shoulders and chest. No bare chest; nothing of a human body shows below the neck.
- A HAG'S HEAD on a long, scrawny neck: grey, leathery skin, a hooked nose, sunken cheeks, a wide mouth of small yellowed pointed teeth, open in a shriek. Two glowing sickly yellow eyes. Long, wild, matted hair the colour of ash, streaming back.
- BIRD LEGS below the feathered hips: scaly, dark, knobbly shins, and big hooked TALONS, four toes each with long curved claws the colour of old rust.
- A short fan of ragged tail feathers below the hips.
- A muted, dusty palette, unlike the imp's red, the acolyte's oxblood or the knight's steel: slate and soot-brown feathers, ash-grey skin and hair, dark scaly legs, rust claws, and sickly yellow only for the eyes.

THE WINGS IN EACH VIEW
- FRONT: both wings raised in a V above and behind the shoulders, their undersides toward the viewer, the clawed wing fingers at the bends on either side of the head.
- FRONT THREE-QUARTER: the near wing seen more from its underside, the far wing partly behind the body, still raised.
- SIDE (profile): both wings raised, seen almost edge-on, overlapping; the long neck, the hooked nose and the hanging talons in profile.
- REAR THREE-QUARTER and BACK: the backs of the wings, darker on top, the matted hair down the back of the neck, the tail fan.

LAYOUT
- One horizontal row of 5 equal cells, read left to right: FRONT (facing the viewer), FRONT THREE-QUARTER, SIDE (profile), REAR THREE-QUARTER, BACK (facing away).
- In the front three-quarter, side and rear three-quarter views the harpy faces IMAGE-LEFT.
- The same figure at the same scale in every cell, in the same hovering pose, with the tips of its talons on the same level in every cell; only the camera angle changes.
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette, glow, cast shadow or floor.
- The cells are separated by thin vertical lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF), each running the full height of the image.
- The whole figure, including both wingtips and the talons, fits inside each cell with a clear magenta margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped.
- No text, labels, numbers, frame borders, loose feathers, dust, motion lines or effects outside the figure.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no pink or purple glow bleeding into the background.
