Pixel-art first-person view for a Doom/Hexen-style dungeon game: the player's own two armoured hands CASTING FIRE MAGIC, seen from the player's eyes, from behind, as in Hexen or Heretic, where the hands sit at the bottom of the screen. One single frame, not a sprite sheet.

REFERENCE IMAGE 1 is the game's existing first-person SWORD frame: a right gauntlet (steel plates, brass rivets, a leather-and-brass cuff, a steel vambrace running off the bottom of the screen). It sets the style: the pixel size, the dark outline weight, the shading, the colours, and the scale of the hand. REFERENCE IMAGE 2 is the same player holding a spear in both hands: the left gauntlet is the right one's mirror twin. Draw exactly these gauntlets, cuffs and vambraces, at the same size as in reference 2. Do not draw the sword or the spear: the hands are empty but for the fire.

WHICH WAY THE HANDS FACE, in every pose: we see the BACKS of the hands. The armoured backs of the gauntlets (their plates and knuckles) face the camera; the palms face away from the viewer, toward the scene ahead; the fingers point up and forward. The fire burns in the palms, on the far side of the hands: it is BEHIND the hands, so it shows rising above the fingertips and through the gaps between the fingers, and never sits on top of a hand or in front of it. The palms themselves are never seen.

THE FIRE: real flames, orange and yellow with a white-hot heart, drawn as crisp pixel art with hard-edged tongues of flame and a few separate embers, as in a classic 1990s game. No smooth glow, no soft halo, no light cast on the background. The fire is the only orange or yellow thing in the picture apart from the gauntlets' small brass rivets.

THE PICTURE
- The whole 1536 x 1024 canvas is the player's screen.
- The camera is the player's eyes: the hands are the nearest things, much bigger than they would be in a third-person view. No body, no shoulders, no head, no legs.
- Both forearms run off the BOTTOM edge of the screen, the left one left of the screen's middle and the right one right of it, never off the side edges.
- The upper half of the screen and its centre stay empty unless the pose says otherwise: it is where the player looks.

STRICT RULES
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere around the hands and fire: no floor, no walls, no gradient, no vignette, no glow, no shadow.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels and no pink or purple glow bleeding into the background.
- No text, labels, frame borders, grid lines, motion blur or effects other than the fire.
