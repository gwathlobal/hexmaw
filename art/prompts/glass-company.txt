
THIS PANEL: a small WINDOW of the Free Company, a band of sell-swords, exactly 1.5 times as tall as it is wide, a plain rectangle with a flat top.
- The design: a heater SHIELD in the middle, split down its centre, the left half crimson and the right half amber gold, with a crossed SWORD and SPEAR behind it, their blades pale steel blue, and below the shield a single round gold COIN.
- The background glass is plain leaded diamond quarries in bottle green and pale green, like a garrison's window.
- A border band of narrow crimson and gold pieces runs round all four sides inside the outer lead.
- Bold and simple, a soldier's emblem: no letters, no faces.
