
THIS PANEL: a ROSE window in a SQUARE panel, exactly as tall as it is wide.
- A large circle fills the square. The four corners of the square outside the circle are filled with SOLID PURE BLACK, as part of the lead.
- The circle is divided by black lead into twelve petal-shaped panes radiating from the centre, in alternating deep violet and ruby red, each with a small gold piece at its outer tip.
- In the very centre, a round medallion about a third of the circle's width: a monstrous FANGED MAW seen from the front, wide open, a ring of pointed ivory-yellow fangs above and below round a deep red throat with an ember-orange glow at its middle, as in the second reference image, the game's title picture of a maw hewn in rock.
- A thin ring of small amber and gold pieces runs round the circle's edge inside the outer lead.
