THE SURFACE: a dungeon WALL of rough-cut grey-brown stone blocks in mortared courses.
- Eight courses of stone from top to bottom, each course the same height, so the pattern repeats exactly vertically. Blocks of varied length within a course (one to two and a half times as long as they are tall), joints staggered between courses like brickwork.
- Stones are cool grey with a brownish cast, a few slightly darker or warmer than the rest; chipped corners, a few hairline cracks, worn faces.
- Mortar joints dark and recessed, about one sixteenth of a course thick.
