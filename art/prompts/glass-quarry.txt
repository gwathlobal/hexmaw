
THIS PANEL: a small plain WINDOW of an old guardhouse, exactly 1.5 times as tall as it is wide, a plain rectangle with a flat top.
- The design: a lattice of DIAMOND-SHAPED panes (quarries) set in thin black lead running diagonally both ways, about four diamonds across and six down, the diamonds at the edges cut off by the outer border.
- The glass is plain and old: most diamonds pale amber and honey yellow, some a pale bottle green, a few a deeper green, each diamond with one lighter streak.
- A border band of narrow amber pieces runs round all four sides inside the outer lead.
- Simpler and humbler than a church window: no figures, no symbols.
