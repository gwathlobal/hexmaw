Pixel-art sprite sheet: the 8-frame death animation of the RIFTWALKER, an assassin who steps through rifts in the air, for a Doom/Hexen-style first-person game where monsters are drawn as sprites. The death is seen from the front only. The last frame is the corpse, which stays on the floor.

REFERENCE IMAGES, in the order attached:
1. The riftwalker's turnaround sheet. It is the character design: a lean, wiry humanoid in a low knife-fighter's crouch, knees bent, weight forward; a close hood and short cowl in charcoal, and under the hood a smooth featureless oval MASK of polished brass with no eyes, mouth or nose; tight wrappings of charcoal and deep indigo round arms, chest and legs, a short split tabard to mid-thigh, wrapped boots; thin cracks of pale silver-blue light along the seams of the wrappings; a long CURVED DAGGER of pale steel in its right hand, held low; a small hard-edged diamond SHARD of pale silver-blue light floating over its open left palm. Also the shading style.
2. The riftwalker standing, seen from the front. Match its pixel size, outline weight and scale exactly: frame 1 is exactly as tall as reference 2.

THE DEATH: the rifts that carried it close on it. Its light goes out and it falls.
- Frame 1, HIT: standing as in reference 2 but jolted back, the mask tipped up, the shard shattering into a few pale silver-blue pieces.
- Frame 2, CRACK: a jagged crack of light splits the brass mask from brow to chin; the cracks along its wrappings flare bright; the arms flung wide, the dagger slipping from its fingers.
- Frame 3, BUCKLING: the light in the cracks dying, it drops to its knees, the dagger falling beside it.
- Frame 4, TOPPLING: kneeling, it pitches forward, one hand out to catch itself.
- Frame 5, FALLING: its body falling forward toward the floor, the hood sliding back.
- Frame 6, DOWN: lying face down on the floor, sprawled, the split mask turned to one side.
- Frame 7, STILL: lying face down, one arm stretched out toward the curved dagger lying on the floor, a last faint glint of light in the cracked mask.
- Frame 8, CORPSE: the same, lying still, all light gone, the mask split in two halves of dull brass. This frame stays on screen.

LAYOUT
- A grid of 2 rows of 4 equal cells: frames 1 to 4 in the top row, 5 to 8 in the bottom row, each read left to right.
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette, glow, cast shadow or floor.
- The cells are separated by thin lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF): vertical lines between the columns, each running the full height of the image, and one horizontal line between the rows, running the full width.
- Each frame follows on smoothly from the one before: it falls in one place, without jumps between frames, and once down it never leaves the floor.
- The same scale in every frame. In every frame the figure rests on the same ground line near the bottom of its cell; nothing reaches below it, and nothing floats.
- Everything fits inside each cell with a clear magenta margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped.
- No text, labels, numbers, frame borders, speed lines, motion blur, dust, sparks, blood pools or gore.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no pink, purple or cyan glow bleeding into the background.
