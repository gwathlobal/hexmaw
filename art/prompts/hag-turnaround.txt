Pixel-art character turnaround sheet for a monster in a Doom/Hexen-style first-person dark-fantasy dungeon game, where monsters are drawn as sprites seen from several camera angles: the RIME HAG, a witch of the frozen caves who throws shards of ice, freezes the floor under her foes and bursts into a nova of frost when they come close.

REFERENCE IMAGES, in the order attached: turnaround sheets of four other monsters from the same game (a red imp, a hooded robed acolyte, a gaunt necromancer with a staff, and a floating wraith of rags), then the game's style anchor sheet (an imp, a knight, a stone wall, a floor, a flask and a sword together). Use them ONLY for the art style and the sheet layout: pixel size, outline weight, shading, how the views are arranged and how far each one is turned. Do not copy anything of their designs. In particular the rime hag must NOT look like the necromancer: no skull on the staff, no beard, no green, no black robes; not like the wraith: she has legs and feet and stands on the floor, no streaming rags, no hidden face; not like the acolyte: no orange, no oxblood, no fire.

THE RIME HAG
- a stooped, hunched old crone of a little under a man's height, bent at the back, leaning her weight on a staff in her left hand; a long cloak and hood of shaggy GREY FUR stiff and frosted white with hoarfrost at its edges; a MANTLE OF ICICLES hanging from her shoulders over the fur like a short cape of pale blue-white spikes; long thin arms and bony hands with BLUE-WHITE SKIN, a gaunt old face under the hood with pale white eyes, long lank white hair; a STAFF OF BLACK ICE, dark and glassy, taller than she is, topped by a FROZEN LANTERN, a small iron cage around a cold glowing core of pale cyan-white light; ragged hems, bare frostbitten feet.
- She stands hunched and leaning on the staff, the lantern held up beside her head, her right hand open and clawed at her side, frost rising off her fingers as a few small solid pale pixels, not a haze.
- A cold palette, unlike the imp's red, the acolyte's oxblood and fire, the necromancer's black and green or the wraith's slate greys and violet: warm grey and ash fur, white frost, pale ice blue and blue-white skin, glassy black-blue for the staff, and bright cyan-white only for the lantern's light.

LAYOUT
- One horizontal row of 5 equal cells, read left to right: FRONT (facing the viewer), FRONT THREE-QUARTER, SIDE (profile), REAR THREE-QUARTER, BACK (facing away).
- In the front three-quarter, side and rear three-quarter views the hag faces IMAGE-LEFT.
- The same figure at the same scale in every cell, her feet on the same ground line, in the same pose; only the camera angle changes.
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette, glow, cast shadow or floor.
- The cells are separated by thin vertical lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF), each running the full height of the image.
- The whole figure, including the staff and its lantern, fits inside each cell with a clear magenta margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped.
- No text, labels, numbers, frame borders, mist, snow, sparks or light rays outside the figure. The lantern's light stays on the lantern, never as a halo on the magenta.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no pink, purple or cyan glow bleeding into the background.
HOUSE STYLE (Hexmaw; attach art/style/anchor.png as a style reference, and say which reference it is):
- Crisp pixel art at the same pixel size as the reference sheet: about 64 pixels per metre for anything in the world, every pixel square and hard-edged, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no soft glow, no semi-transparent pixels, no dithering gradients.
- Lit from the upper left: lighter top-left faces, darker bottom-right ones, a few hard-edged tones per material, the same contrast as the imp and the knight on the reference.
- Colours from the reference's palette: warm grey-brown stone, dark iron, muted earth tones, and bright saturated colour only for fire, magic and blood.
- Outlines as on the reference: the shape's edge a dark tone of what it encloses, not a uniform black line.
