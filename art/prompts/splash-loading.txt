
THE SCENE: THE LOADING SCREEN, shown while a new dungeon level is being built
- A narrow, steep stone stairway descending away from the viewer into the dark depths of a dungeon, seen from the top step. Rough grey stone walls close in on both sides, an arched stone ceiling above.
- The stairs fall away into pitch black fog at the centre of the picture: that dark depth is the calm centre where the game writes "loading".
- On the LEFT wall near the viewer, an iron wall torch blazing orange, lighting the nearest steps and the wall; on the RIGHT wall, further down, a second torch, smaller with distance.
- Far below in the darkness, the faintest pair of ember-orange eyes, and a red glow of something waiting: only a hint, small and dim.
- In the bottom foreground, on the top step, a few scattered things a delver leaves: a rusty dagger lying on the stone, a skull, a burnt-out candle stub, a splash of dried blood.
- No people, no monsters in full view.
