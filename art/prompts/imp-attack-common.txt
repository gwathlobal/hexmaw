- No text, labels, numbers, frame borders, speed lines, motion blur, smear trails, sparks, blood or impact effects. Only the imp.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no pink or purple glow bleeding into the background.

THE ATTACK: a vicious overhead claw swipe with one arm, as in references 3 and 4.
- Frame 1, WIND-UP: the imp crouches slightly with knees bent and leans back a little. The swinging arm is cocked high above the head, claws spread and hooked. The other arm hangs low and forward for balance. Mouth open in a fanged snarl, tail raised.
