The release. Both hands thrust forward and up toward the centre of the screen, backs to the camera, palms pushing away from the viewer, the fingers spread wide, the heels of the hands together and the two thumbs meeting tip to tip in the middle, under the fire, the four fingers of each hand fanned up and outward. Beyond them, a burst of fire blasts away from the palms toward the centre of the screen: a bright explosion of flame bigger than the hands, white-hot in its middle, tongues of fire and embers flying outward past the fingers.
