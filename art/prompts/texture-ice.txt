THE SURFACE: a dungeon FLOOR of flagstones under a sheet of FROZEN ICE, seen from straight above.
- A clear but cloudy layer of old ice covering the whole image. Through it the large flagstones and their joints show faintly, blurred and paler, about four slabs across the image in each direction.
- The ice is pale blue-grey and steel blue, cold and a little desaturated, with milky white frost patches and a few long hairline cracks that wander across it and continue over the edges.
- Fine pale scratches and scuffs in the ice's surface, as if things had slid over it. A few trapped air bubbles as tiny pale dots.
- Medium-dark overall, not white or glaring: it must sit beside brown-grey stone floors in a torch-lit dungeon without shining.
