THE HEALING
- Eight to twelve small GREEN PLUS SIGNS (+, equal arms, like a healing cross) scattered over and around a tall narrow area the size of a standing body, rising as if the body gives them off: a few bigger pluses about the size of a hand, most small, a few tiny ones, plus single-pixel green sparkles.
- Each plus is solid pixel art: a bright pale-green highlight, a clear leaf green body and a deep green edge, the same look in every frame. No text, no hearts, no other shapes.
- NOT a ring, a column or a glow round the body: the middle is mostly open magenta, so the creature shows through between the pluses.
- The 8 frames loop seamlessly: the pluses float slowly upward, gently swaying, the ones near the top shrinking and fading out as new ones appear near the body's middle and feet; sparkles twinkle. No frame is a single big flash.

LAYOUT
- A grid of 2 rows of 4 equal cells: frames 1 to 4 in the top row, 5 to 8 in the bottom row, each read left to right.
- In every cell the aura is centred and covers the same area, at the same scale.
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette, glow or haze.
- The cells are separated by thin lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF): vertical lines between the columns, each running the full height of the image, and one horizontal line between the rows, running the full width.
- Everything fits inside each cell with a clear magenta margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped.
- No creature, no text, labels, numbers or frame borders.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no glow bleeding into the background.
