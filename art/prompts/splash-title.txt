
THE SCENE: THE MAIN MENU
- Inside a vast, ancient vaulted dungeon hall of rough grey stone blocks, seen from the floor looking down its length. Massive stone pillars on both sides recede into the darkness, and the far end is lost in black fog: that dark depth is the calm centre.
- In the lower foreground, at the centre bottom, the RITUAL DAGGER: a long, wicked ritual dagger with a blackened iron blade, plunged point-down into a cracked stone altar block. Its blade is etched with glowing ember-orange sigils (abstract marks, not letters), and a sickly red-orange glow seeps from the cracks around it. It is the brightest thing in the picture, and it is below the centre, not in it.
- On the LEFT, half in torchlight against a pillar, the red IMP from the reference crouches, claws raised, grinning at the viewer. Behind it a little, the hooded ACOLYTE from the reference, ember eyes glowing in its cowl, a small fireball in its raised palm.
- On the RIGHT, the KNIGHT from the reference: horned helm, blackened plate, the tall steel tower shield with glowing runes held before it, a mace in its other hand. High above it, near the top right under the vault, the HARPY from the reference, wings spread, swooping.
- Two iron wall torches on the pillars, one on each side, throwing warm orange light on the monsters nearest them. Blood stains on the floor flagstones, a few bones and a skull.
- The monsters are menacing but small enough that each is whole in the picture; none crosses into the calm centre.
