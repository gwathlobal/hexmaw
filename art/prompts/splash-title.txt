
THE SCENE: THE MAIN MENU
- Inside a vast, ancient vaulted dungeon hall of rough grey stone blocks, seen from the floor looking down its length. Massive stone pillars on both sides recede toward the far end.
- THE MAW: the whole far end of the hall is one colossal mouth hewn into the rock, as tall as the vault. Huge jagged fangs of grey stone hang from its upper lip and jut up from its lower one, framing an opening as wide as the middle third of the picture. Inside it is a throat of pure black darkness going down and away: that black throat IS the calm centre where the menu sits, so nothing inside it is lit or detailed. Along the stone lips, outside the throat, a few faint ember-red hex sigils are carved and glow dimly (abstract angular marks, not letters). The hall's flagstone floor runs on into the mouth as a broad worn stair that sinks into the throat, like a tongue, its lowest steps lost in the dark.
- The maw is carved, not alive: no eyes, no flesh, no face. It reads as a mouth only by its shape and its teeth.
- On the LEFT, half in torchlight against a pillar, the red IMP from the reference crouches, claws raised, grinning at the viewer. Behind it a little, the hooded ACOLYTE from the reference, ember eyes glowing in its cowl, a small fireball in its raised palm.
- On the RIGHT, the KNIGHT from the reference: horned helm, blackened plate, the tall steel tower shield with glowing runes held before it, a mace in its other hand. High above it, near the top right under the vault, the HARPY from the reference, wings spread, swooping.
- Two iron wall torches on the pillars, one on each side, throwing warm orange light on the monsters nearest them; they are the brightest things in the picture. Blood stains on the floor flagstones, a few bones and a skull, a trail of them leading up the stair into the mouth.
- The monsters are menacing but small enough that each is whole in the picture; none crosses into the black throat.
