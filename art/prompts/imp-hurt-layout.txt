
The imp has exactly ONE tail, growing from the base of its spine, with one arrowhead tip. Count it in every frame: never draw a second tail, a second arrowhead, or the tail in two places at once.

LAYOUT
- One horizontal row of 2 equal cells, read left to right, each cell the width of one cell of reference 2.
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette, glow, cast shadow or floor.
- The two cells are separated by one thin vertical line of pure cyan (#00FFFF), running the full height of the image.
- The imp is drawn at the same scale in both cells, as large as in reference 2 (were it standing upright it would be exactly as tall).
- The feet stand on the same ground line in both cells, at the same height as in reference 2. The imp does not hop or float.
- The whole body, including flung-out claws and the tail tip, fits inside each cell with a clear magenta margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or the cyan line, and nothing is cropped.
- No text, labels, numbers, frame borders, speed lines, motion blur, sparks, blood, wounds, burns, smoke or impact effects. Only the imp; the game adds the hit flash itself.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no pink or purple glow bleeding into the background.

THE FLINCH: a sharp, readable reaction to being hit in the chest from in front of the imp, in the spirit of a Doom pain frame. The body is knocked backward away from the direction it faces.
- Frame 1, JOLT: the instant of the hit. The head snaps back and the chin goes up, eyes squeezed into slits, mouth wide open in a shriek showing the fangs. The chest caves in and the shoulders jerk up toward the ears. Both arms fly up and out to the sides at shoulder height, elbows bent, claws splayed open. The upper body bends backward, away from the direction the imp faces; the knees buckle and the feet stay planted. The tail whips up stiff behind it.
- Frame 2, STAGGER: a moment later. The imp doubles over forward, hunched low, head ducked down between the shoulders and wincing, teeth clenched. One clawed hand clutches its chest; the other arm hangs out loose for balance. Knees deeply bent, one foot shuffled half a step back. The tail droops low. This must read as clearly hurt, not as an attack.
