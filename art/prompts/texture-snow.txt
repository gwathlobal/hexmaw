THE SURFACE: the FLOOR of an ice cave, seen from straight above: hard-packed, trodden SNOW and hoarfrost over rock.
- Grey-white and pale blue-grey packed snow, scuffed and trampled, with shallow footprint dents and drag marks, small crusts and clumps.
- Here and there dark rock and grit showing through thin patches, and glittering frost crystals as tiny pale specks.
- No slabs, no tiles, no straight joints, no paving: nothing laid by hand.
- SECOND REFERENCE: the game's ice floor, which this snow lies beside. Keep to its cold palette, but matt and rough, not glassy.
- Muted and a little grimy, of medium brightness, not white or glaring: torch-lit, it must not shine.
- Evenly spread over the whole image, with no big feature at the centre, so it tiles.
