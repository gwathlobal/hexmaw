Pixel-art character turnaround sheet for a monster in a Doom/Hexen-style first-person dark-fantasy dungeon game, where monsters are drawn as sprites seen from several camera angles: the NECROMANCER, a withered sorcerer who keeps his distance, raises skeletons from the bodies of the fallen, and hurls glowing green skulls.

REFERENCE IMAGES, in the order attached: turnaround sheets of four other monsters from the same game (a red imp, a hooded robed acolyte, an armoured knight, and a winged harpy). Use them ONLY for the art style and the sheet layout: pixel size, outline weight, shading, how the views are arranged and how far each one is turned. Do not copy anything of their designs. In particular the necromancer must NOT look like the acolyte: no hood, no cowl, no fire, no orange or oxblood.

THE NECROMANCER
- A tall, gaunt, stooped old man, about as tall as the acolyte, leaning a little forward. Painfully thin: the cheekbones, collarbones and the tendons of the hands show through the skin.
- BAREHEADED: a bald, bone-pale skull-like head with sunken cheeks, a long thin grey beard hanging to the chest, deep dark eye sockets with two small GLOWING EYES, sickly bright green.
- Skin the colour of old bone or candle wax, faintly grey-green in the shadows.
- RAGGED ROBES in layers of black and dark sickly green, torn into tatters at the hem and the sleeves, with a high stiff collar standing up behind the head. A belt of rope hung with small bones and a little skull. The robe stops at the shins: below it the BARE, BONY FEET stand on the ground, so he walks rather than glides.
- A TALL STAFF in his RIGHT hand, standing on the ground beside him and rising well above his head: a twisted, blackened wooden shaft, crowned with a HUMAN SKULL bound on with cord, its eye sockets glowing the same sickly green as his eyes. The staff and its skull are the most visible part of his silhouette.
- His LEFT hand is raised a little in front of him, fingers long and clawed, curled as if holding something unseen.
- A cold, deathly palette, unlike the imp's red, the acolyte's oxblood and fire, the knight's steel or the harpy's dusty browns: black, dark sickly green, bone white, grey, and bright sickly green only for the glowing eyes and the staff skull's sockets.

LAYOUT
- One horizontal row of 5 equal cells, read left to right: FRONT (facing the viewer), FRONT THREE-QUARTER, SIDE (profile), REAR THREE-QUARTER, BACK (facing away).
- In the front three-quarter, side and rear three-quarter views the necromancer faces IMAGE-LEFT.
- The staff stays in the same hand (the right) in every view, so it is on the opposite side of the body in the front and back views.
- The same figure at the same scale in every cell, standing on the same ground line, in the same pose; only the camera angle changes.
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette, glow, cast shadow or floor.
- The cells are separated by thin vertical lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF), each running the full height of the image.
- The whole figure, including the top of the staff, fits inside each cell with a clear magenta margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped.
- No text, labels, numbers, frame borders, smoke, sparks or effects outside the figure. The green glow stays in the eyes and the staff skull's sockets, never as a halo on the magenta.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no pink or purple glow bleeding into the background.
