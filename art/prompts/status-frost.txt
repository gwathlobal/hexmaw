Pixel-art sprite sheet for a Doom/Hexen-style first-person dark-fantasy dungeon game: an 8-frame looping FROST AURA, the cold that clings to a monster frozen by a frost spell. It is drawn over the monster's body as an overlay, so draw only the frost, never a creature.

THE FROST
- A tall, narrow column of cold about one and a half times as tall as it is wide, the shape of a standing body's outline: jagged shards of rime and hoarfrost crusting its lower edges and sides, a thin veil of pale frost mist across its middle, and small sharp ice crystals and snow motes drifting slowly upward around it.
- The middle is mostly open magenta: the frost sits round the edges and in scattered crystals, so the monster shows through it.
- Colours: white highlights, pale ice blue, a mid cold blue and a few deep blue shadow pixels. No yellow, orange or green.
- The 8 frames loop seamlessly: the crystals and motes drift up a little each frame and fade out at the top as new ones appear near the bottom, the rime twinkles (a highlight moves from shard to shard), the mist wavers. The rime's outline stays in the same place in every frame. No single bright flash or burst frame.

LAYOUT
- A grid of 2 rows of 4 equal cells: frames 1 to 4 in the top row, 5 to 8 in the bottom row, each read left to right.
- In every cell the aura is centred and stands on the same baseline near the bottom of the cell, at the same scale.
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette, glow or haze.
- The cells are separated by thin lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF): vertical lines between the columns, each running the full height of the image, and one horizontal line between the rows, running the full width.
- Everything fits inside each cell with a clear magenta margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped.
- No creature, no text, labels, numbers or frame borders.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no glow bleeding into the background.
