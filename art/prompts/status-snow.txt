Pixel-art sprite sheet for a Doom/Hexen-style first-person dark-fantasy dungeon game: an 8-frame looping FROST AURA, the cold that pours off a monster frozen by a frost spell. It is drawn over the monster's body as an overlay, so draw only the frost, never a creature.

REFERENCE IMAGES, in the order attached:
1. A finished aura sheet from the same game: crackling lightning over a body. Copy its layout, scale and density exactly: 2 rows of 4 cells, the same tall narrow area covered, the same amount of open magenta, many small separate pieces scattered over the area rather than one big shape. This one is snow and ice instead of lightning.
2. An earlier frost sheet from the same game. Use only its ice colours: white, pale ice blue, a mid cold blue and a few deep blue pixels.

THE FROST
- Ten to fifteen small SNOWFLAKES and ice crystals scattered over and around a tall narrow area the size of a standing body, as if the frozen body gives them off: six-pointed snowflakes a few pixels across, tiny diamond-shaped ice crystals, and single-pixel frost sparkles. A few bigger snowflakes, most small.
- NOT a crust, a ring or an outline round the body, and no mist: the middle is mostly open magenta, so the monster shows through between the flakes.
- The 8 frames loop seamlessly: the flakes drift slowly up and outward from the body, turning and twinkling (a sparkle moves from flake to flake), the ones at the top and sides fading out as new ones appear near the body's middle and feet. No frame is a single big flash.

LAYOUT
- A grid of 2 rows of 4 equal cells: frames 1 to 4 in the top row, 5 to 8 in the bottom row, each read left to right.
- In every cell the aura is centred and covers the same area, at the same scale.
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette, glow or haze.
- The cells are separated by thin lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF): vertical lines between the columns, each running the full height of the image, and one horizontal line between the rows, running the full width.
- Everything fits inside each cell with a clear magenta margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped.
- No creature, no text, labels, numbers or frame borders.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no glow bleeding into the background.
