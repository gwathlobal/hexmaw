Pixel-art sprite sheet of a spell effect for a Doom/Hexen-style first-person dark-fantasy game, where effects are drawn as small flat sprites: the RIFT FLASH, what is left in the air for a moment where a body steps through a rift and vanishes, or steps out of one. It is drawn the size of a person, standing upright.

REFERENCE IMAGE: the sheet of a spell from the same game (an orange fire bolt and its burst). Match its art style exactly: pixel size, outline weight, how a burst grows and breaks up. Use it ONLY for the style; do not copy its colours, its fire or its layout. This is not fire: no flames, no smoke.

THE FRAMES, 6 in all, a short burst:
- Frame 1: a tall, narrow, jagged VERTICAL CRACK of blinding white light, as tall as a person, thin as a blade, with short jagged branches at its ends.
- Frame 2: the crack torn wide open into a tall jagged diamond, pitch-black indigo inside, its rim bright white and ice blue, jagged cracks of light spreading out from its edges.
- Frame 3: the diamond snapping shut, narrower, its cracks flying outward as broken shards of light.
- Frame 4: only a thin bright line left where it was, and shards of light drifting outward and down.
- Frame 5: a fading line and a few shards.
- Frame 6: a handful of bright specks.

COLOURS: pure white in the brightest parts, pale ice blue, a deep royal blue at the edges, near-black indigo inside the open rift. No orange, yellow, red, green, magenta or purple.

LAYOUT
- One horizontal row of 6 equal tall cells, read left to right, each about twice as tall as wide.
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette or glow.
- The cells are separated by thin vertical lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF), each running the full height of the image.
- Each frame is centred in its cell with a clear magenta margin; nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped. Every frame is centred on the same point.
- No text, labels, numbers or frame borders.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no glow bleeding into the background: the light is drawn as solid pixels on the effect only.
