Pixel-art sprite sheet: the pain (flinch) animation of the ARC VOTARY, a blind monk who holds a beam of lightning between its hands and turns it slowly on its enemies, for a Doom/Hexen-style first-person game where monsters are drawn as sprites from several camera angles.

REFERENCE IMAGES, in the order attached:
1. The votary's turnaround sheet (front, front three-quarter, side, rear three-quarter, back). It is the character design: a tall, gaunt humanoid of a man's height, as tall as the acolyte, standing upright and still, slightly stooped from the shoulders; a shaven head, bare and pale, its EYES SEWN SHUT, a row of dark stitches across each closed lid; a long ankle-length monk's HABIT of undyed pale linen gone grey-brown with grime, with COPPER WIRE stitched through it in bright lines and spirals down the chest, sleeves and hem; a heavy dark LODESTONE, a rough lump of black-grey iron stone, hanging on two iron chains at the middle of its chest; copper bands round both wrists; bare feet under the hem; its two HANDS held apart in front of the chest, palms facing each other a forearm's length apart, with a few pale yellow-white SPARKS jumping between them. Also the shading style.
2. The votary standing, seen from THIS sheet's camera view. Match its camera angle, the way it faces, its pixel size, outline weight, scale and ground line exactly.

It has two arms and two legs, no more, and no weapon. Its eyes stay sewn shut. Any lightning is pale yellow-white with a white core, drawn as solid hard-edged pixels on or right next to the figure, never a soft halo on the magenta.

LAYOUT
- One horizontal row of 3 equal cells, read left to right, each cell as wide as reference 2: cell 1 the standing copy, cells 2 to 3 the 2 frames.
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette, glow, cast shadow or floor.
- The cells are separated by thin vertical lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF), each running the full height of the image.
- Cell 1 is a plain copy of reference 2: the votary standing, exactly as tall, in exactly the same pose. It sets the scale for the other frames, which are drawn at the same scale.
- The feet stay on the same ground line in every cell, at the same height as in reference 2.
- The whole figure, lightning included, fits inside each cell with a clear magenta margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped.
- No text, labels, numbers, frame borders, speed lines, motion blur, smear trails, dust or light rays. Only the votary.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no pink, purple or cyan glow bleeding into the background.

THE FLINCH: a sharp, readable reaction to a blow, in the spirit of a Doom pain frame. It plays for a third of a second when it is struck by a sword or a fire bolt. It must read clearly as hurt, not as an attack.
- Cell 2, JOLT: the instant of the hit. The head snapped back, the mouth open, the chest thrust back, both hands flung apart and out, the sparks scattering off the fingers; the knees buckling.
- Cell 3, STAGGER: a moment later. It doubles forward, lower than standing, both hands clutching the lodestone at its chest, the head bowed, no sparks.
