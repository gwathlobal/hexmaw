
THIS PANEL: a tall LANCET window, exactly 2.5 times as tall as it is wide.
- The top ends in a pointed gothic arch. The two top corners of the rectangle outside the arch are filled with SOLID PURE BLACK, as part of the lead.
- The design: a large six-armed FROST STAR, its arms like ice crystals, in pale ice blue and cyan, in the upper half; below it three rows of stylised waves in cobalt and deep blue, rising toward the star.
- The background glass is a deep cobalt blue cut into small irregular pieces, with a few pieces of pale teal.
- A narrow border band of alternating small blue and violet pieces runs up both sides inside the outer lead.
