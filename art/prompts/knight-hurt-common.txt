Pixel-art sprite sheet: the pain (flinch) animation of the KNIGHT, a slow, heavily armoured warrior behind a huge rune-carved tower shield, for a Doom/Hexen-style first-person game where monsters are drawn as sprites from several camera angles. It plays for a third of a second when the knight is struck by a sword or a fire bolt.

REFERENCE IMAGES, in the order attached:
1. The knight's turnaround sheet (front, front three-quarter, side, rear three-quarter, back). It is the character design: a broad humanoid in blackened, angular fantasy plate with spiked pauldrons, a closed HORNED HELM with two glowing pale-blue eyes in its narrow slit, steel tassets over dark chainmail, an all-metal TOWER SHIELD as tall as the knight (riveted rim, a central ridge, columns of carved runes glowing faint pale blue) on its LEFT arm, and a heavy flanged, spiked steel mace in its RIGHT hand. Also the shading style.
2. The knight standing, seen from THIS sheet's camera view. Match its camera angle, the way it faces, its pixel size, outline weight, scale and ground line exactly.

The shield stays on the left arm and the mace in the right hand in every frame. Never swap them, never draw a second shield, a second mace or a third arm. The horns, the glowing eyes and the runes look the same in every frame.

LAYOUT
- One horizontal row of 3 equal cells, read left to right, each cell as wide as reference 2: cell 1 the standing copy, cells 2 and 3 the 2 pain frames.
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette, glow, cast shadow or floor.
- The cells are separated by thin vertical lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF), each running the full height of the image.
- Cell 1 is a plain copy of reference 2: the knight standing, exactly as tall, in exactly the same pose. It sets the scale for the other frames, which are drawn at the same scale.
- The feet stand on the same ground line in every cell, at the same height as in reference 2. The knight does not hop or float.
- The whole figure, including the horns, the shield and the mace, fits inside each cell with a clear magenta margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped.
- No text, labels, numbers, frame borders, speed lines, motion blur, smear trails, sparks, dust, blood or impact effects. Only the knight.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no pink or purple glow bleeding into the background.

THE FLINCH: a sharp, readable reaction to a heavy blow, in the spirit of a Doom pain frame. The knight is heavy, so it rocks rather than being thrown, and its feet stay planted. It must read clearly as hurt, not as an attack or a block.
- Cell 2, JOLT: the instant of the hit. The helm snaps back and to one side, the glowing eyes flaring wide and bright, the shoulders jerk up, the upper body rocks back; the shield arm swings out a little from the body and the mace arm flies out to the side.
- Cell 3, STAGGER: a moment later. The knight hunches forward and down, the helm bowed, the eyes narrowed, the shield sagging so its top dips, the mace arm hanging low. A little lower than standing.
