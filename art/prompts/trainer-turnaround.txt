Pixel-art character turnaround sheet for a Doom/Hexen-style first-person dark-fantasy dungeon game, where characters are drawn as sprites seen from several camera angles: the TRAINER, a veteran fighter who drills the folk of a grim walled settlement deep underground.

REFERENCE IMAGES, in the order attached:
1. The trainer, seen from the front. It is the character design: copy it exactly, the same man, clothes, colours and gear, only turned for each view.
2. and 3. Turnaround sheets of two monsters from the same game (a red imp, a hooded acolyte). Use them ONLY for the art style and the sheet layout: pixel size, outline weight, shading, how the views are arranged and how far each one is turned. Do not copy anything of their designs.

THE TRAINER (as in reference 1)
- A broad-shouldered, heavily muscled veteran of average height, past his prime, close-cropped grey hair, a hard scarred face, old scars on his bare arms, standing square and steady.
- A sleeveless quilted brown padded gambeson closed with brass buckles down the front, a brown leather belt; dark leather bracers on both forearms; baggy dark brown trousers tucked into strapped leather boots.
- A plain WOODEN PRACTICE SWORD (a long wooden waster with a cross-guard and pommel, pale golden-brown wood, no metal) standing point-down on the ground in front of him, both hands resting on its pommel at his belt, as in reference 1.
- The same pose in every view: in the turned views the sword is still in front of him, seen from the side, its point on the ground ahead of his feet; in the back view it is hidden behind his body but for its point between his feet.

LAYOUT
- One horizontal row of 5 equal cells, read left to right: FRONT (facing the viewer), FRONT THREE-QUARTER, SIDE (profile), REAR THREE-QUARTER, BACK (facing away).
- In the front three-quarter, side and rear three-quarter views the figure faces IMAGE-LEFT.
- The same figure at the same scale in every cell, standing on the same ground line, in the same pose; only the camera angle changes.
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette, glow, cast shadow or floor.
- The cells are separated by thin vertical lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF), each running the full height of the image.
- The whole figure fits inside each cell with a clear magenta margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped.
- No text, labels, numbers, frame borders or effects outside the figure.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no pink or purple glow bleeding into the background.
