THE SURFACE: SOLID DRESSED STONE, as a carved sarcophagus or altar is made of: one smooth monolithic face of pale cold grey stone, with no masonry courses and no blocks.
- Faint parallel chisel tooling marks, subtle mottling in the stone, a few darker weathered patches and hairline cracks, small chips. All of it small and spread evenly over the whole image.
- Pale grey with a faint green-grey cast, lighter than a dungeon wall, low contrast, so it reads as one block of fine stone.
