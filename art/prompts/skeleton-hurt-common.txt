Pixel-art sprite sheet: the pain (flinch) animation of the SKELETON, a dead warrior raised by a necromancer, for a Doom/Hexen-style first-person game where monsters are drawn as sprites from several camera angles.

REFERENCE IMAGES, in the order attached:
1. The skeleton's turnaround sheet (front, front three-quarter, side, rear three-quarter, back). It is the character design: a bare human skeleton of a tall man standing hunched, knees a little bent; old yellowed bone stained brown in the joints and cracks; a grinning skull with two small glowing sickly green eyes deep in the sockets; a rotted dark leather belt at the hips with a torn strip of dark cloth hanging from it; a dented, rusted iron guard on the LEFT forearm; and in its RIGHT hand an old SWORD with a notched, rusty blade. No flesh, skin or organs anywhere. Also the shading style.
2. The skeleton standing, seen from THIS sheet's camera view. Match its camera angle, the way it faces, its pixel size, outline weight, scale and ground line exactly.

The sword stays in the right hand and the arm guard on the left forearm in every frame. Never swap them, never draw a second sword or a third arm. The skull, its green eyes, the belt and the cloth look the same in every frame.

LAYOUT
- One horizontal row of 3 equal cells, read left to right, each cell as wide as reference 2: cell 1 the standing copy, cells 2 to 3 the 2 frames.
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette, glow, cast shadow or floor.
- The cells are separated by thin vertical lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF), each running the full height of the image.
- Cell 1 is a plain copy of reference 2: the skeleton standing, exactly as tall, in exactly the same pose. It sets the scale for the other frames, which are drawn at the same scale.
- The feet stand on the same ground line in every cell, at the same height as in reference 2. It does not hop or float.
- The whole figure, including the sword, fits inside each cell with a clear magenta margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped.
- No text, labels, numbers, frame borders, speed lines, motion blur, smear trails, sparks, dust, blood or impact effects. Only the skeleton.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no pink or purple glow bleeding into the background.

THE FLINCH: a sharp, readable reaction to a blow, in the spirit of a Doom pain frame. It plays for a third of a second when the skeleton is struck by a sword or a fire bolt. It must read clearly as hurt, not as an attack. Its feet stay planted.
- Cell 2, JOLT: the instant of the hit. The skull knocked back and to one side, the jaw flung open, the rib cage twisted, the bones jarred a little apart at the joints (small gaps at the neck, shoulders and hips), both arms thrown out, the sword flung wide.
- Cell 3, STAGGER: a moment later. The bones snapped back together; it hunches forward and lower than standing, the skull bowed, the sword arm hanging low.
