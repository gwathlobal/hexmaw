
THE SHIELD: a large kite shield, the classic Hexen fighter's look: steel face, brass rim and studs, a faded red field. It is BIGGER in the frame than a low ready pose: it fills most of the bottom-left quarter of the screen, its top edge reaching about a third of the way up the screen, held at a slant with its top leaning toward the middle. We see mostly its back and its thick rim, the gauntlet gripping its strap, and a sliver of its red front face along the edge toward the middle.
