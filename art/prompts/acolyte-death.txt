Pixel-art sprite sheet: the 8-frame death animation of the ACOLYTE, a hooded cultist spellcaster, for a Doom/Hexen-style first-person game where monsters are drawn as sprites. The death is seen from the front only. The last frame is the corpse, which stays on the floor.

REFERENCE IMAGES, in the order attached:
1. The acolyte's turnaround sheet. THE DEATH: the fire inside the acolyte gets loose and burns it away, leaving its empty robe. The body is never shown; under the cowl there is only blackness and fire.
