THE SURFACE: dressed reddish SANDSTONE masonry, as on the pillars of a dungeon hall.
- Neat rectangular ashlar blocks in four equal courses from top to bottom, each block twice as long as it is tall, joints staggered by half a block between courses, so the pattern repeats exactly.
- Stones a muted brick-red to rust-brown sandstone, each block a slightly different shade, faces finely chipped with a lighter worn top edge and a darker bottom edge.
- Thin dark joints.
