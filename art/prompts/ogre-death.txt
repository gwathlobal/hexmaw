Pixel-art sprite sheet: the 8-frame death animation of the OGRE, a hulking brute with a two-handed stone maul, for a Doom/Hexen-style first-person game where monsters are drawn as sprites. The death is seen from the front only. The last frame is the corpse, which stays on the floor.

REFERENCE IMAGES, in the order attached:
1. The ogre's turnaround sheet. It is the character design: a huge, hulking brute, hunched forward, much broader than a man, with massive rounded shoulders, a barrel chest, a heavy gut, arms thick as tree trunks hanging to its knees and short bowed legs; warty grey-green hide, mottled darker on the back, with old red scars; a small head set low between the shoulders, lank black hair, a heavy underbite jaw with two thick yellow tusks, small yellow eyes; riveted iron bands round both wrists and the upper left arm, a chain with an iron ring hanging across the chest; a ragged brown hide loincloth on a belt, hide strips wound round the shins; and a two-handed STONE MAUL, a grey stone block bound with iron cross-bands on a thick wooden haft ringed with iron, held in its RIGHT hand. Also the shading style.

THE DEATH: the great body gives out and comes down like a felled tree.

- Frame 3, BUCKLING: the knees give way, the body sagging lower, the maul falling from its hand and toppling to image-left.

- Frame 6, FALLING: it falls face down from that same spot, crashing onto its belly and arms.
- Frame 7, STILL: it lies face down on the floor, seen from the front: the top of the head and the hunched, mottled back and shoulders toward the viewer, the arms sprawled to the sides, the maul on the floor beside it.
- Frame 8, CORPSE: the same body lying still. This frame stays on screen.

LAYOUT
- A grid of 2 rows of 4 equal cells: frames 1 to 4 in the top row, 5 to 8 in the bottom row, each read left to right.
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette, glow, cast shadow or floor.
- The cells are separated by thin lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF): vertical lines between the columns, each running the full height of the image, and one horizontal line between the rows, running the full width.
- Each frame follows on smoothly from the one before: it falls in one place, without jumps between frames, and once down it never leaves the floor.
- The same scale in every frame. In every frame the figure or what is left of it rests on the same ground line near the bottom of its cell; nothing reaches below it, and nothing floats.
- Everything fits inside each cell with a clear magenta margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped.
- No text, labels, numbers, frame borders, speed lines, motion blur, dust, sparks, blood pools or gore.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no pink or purple glow bleeding into the background.
