
THE SCENE: THE VICTORY SCREEN, shown when the player has slain the boss at the bottom of the dungeon and the run is won
- A vast, high pillared hall deep under the earth, seen from a little above the floor: rough grey stone blocks, two massive pillars rising out of the top of the picture, the far end of the hall lost in black fog.
- The calm dark area is bigger than usual here: the middle third of the picture's width must stay deep shadow and black fog over nearly its whole height, since the game writes a title, several lines of text and a button there. No faces, no bright light and no important detail in it. Only dark flagstones at its very bottom.
- At the LOWER RIGHT, the slain boss: a GIANT version of the red IMP from the reference, three times a man's height, sprawled dead on its back across the flagstones, one huge clawed arm flung out, its horned head turned away and lolling. Faint embers and a last dull crimson glow are dying out along its skin and in cracks in the floor under it, the light going out of it. A wide dark pool of blood beneath it. It is whole in the picture and does not cross into the calm centre.
- At the LOWER LEFT, the delver who killed it, standing and alive, seen from behind and a little to the side, armoured, no visible face: the sword from the sword reference held low in the right gauntlet, its blade notched and wet, and the steel-rimmed round-topped shield from the shield reference still on the left arm, dented and scored. Shoulders square, weary but unbroken, looking toward the fallen giant.
- On the left pillar above the delver, an iron wall torch from the torch reference burning steadily: a warm orange flame lighting the delver's armour and the nearest stones.
- A thin shaft of cold pale light falls from very high above onto the delver alone, as if from a crack far up in the vault: the only cool colour in the picture, small and narrow, not crossing the calm centre.
- The mood is grim triumph: the long descent is over, the hall is silent, and the delver is the one left standing.
