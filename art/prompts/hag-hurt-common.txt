Pixel-art sprite sheet: the pain (flinch) animation of the RIME HAG, a witch of the frozen caves who throws shards of ice, freezes the floor under her foes and bursts into a nova of frost when they come close, for a Doom/Hexen-style first-person game where monsters are drawn as sprites from several camera angles.

REFERENCE IMAGES, in the order attached:
1. The rime hag's turnaround sheet (front, front three-quarter, side, rear three-quarter, back). It is the character design: a stooped, hunched old crone of a little under a man's height, bent at the back, leaning her weight on a staff in her left hand; a long cloak and hood of shaggy GREY FUR stiff and frosted white with hoarfrost at its edges; a MANTLE OF ICICLES hanging from her shoulders over the fur like a short cape of pale blue-white spikes; long thin arms and bony hands with BLUE-WHITE SKIN, a gaunt old face under the hood with pale white eyes, long lank white hair; a STAFF OF BLACK ICE, dark and glassy, taller than she is, topped by a FROZEN LANTERN, a small iron cage around a cold glowing core of pale cyan-white light; ragged hems, bare frostbitten feet. Also the shading style.
2. The rime hag standing, seen from THIS sheet's camera view. Match its camera angle, the way she faces, her pixel size, outline weight, scale and ground line exactly.
(A further reference may follow; the game's style anchor sheet is the LAST reference, for the style only.)

She has two arms and two legs, no more. The staff stays in her left hand in every frame. Any frost or cold light is pale blue-white or cyan-white, drawn as solid pixels on or right next to her, never a soft halo on the magenta.

LAYOUT
- One horizontal row of 3 equal cells, read left to right, each cell as wide as reference 2: cell 1 the standing copy, cells 2 to 3 the 2 frames.
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette, glow, cast shadow or floor.
- The cells are separated by thin vertical lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF), each running the full height of the image.
- Cell 1 is a plain copy of reference 2: the hag standing, exactly as tall, in exactly the same pose. It sets the scale for the other frames, which are drawn at the same scale.
- The feet stay on the same ground line in every cell, at the same height as in reference 2.
- The whole figure, including the staff and its lantern, fits inside each cell with a clear magenta margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped.
- No text, labels, numbers, frame borders, speed lines, motion blur, smear trails, mist, snow or light rays. Only the rime hag.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no pink, purple or cyan glow bleeding into the background.

HOUSE STYLE (Hexmaw; attach art/style/anchor.png as a style reference, and say which reference it is):
- Crisp pixel art at the same pixel size as the reference sheet: about 64 pixels per metre for anything in the world, every pixel square and hard-edged, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no soft glow, no semi-transparent pixels, no dithering gradients.
- Lit from the upper left: lighter top-left faces, darker bottom-right ones, a few hard-edged tones per material, the same contrast as the imp and the knight on the reference.
- Colours from the reference's palette: warm grey-brown stone, dark iron, muted earth tones, and bright saturated colour only for fire, magic and blood.
- Outlines as on the reference: the shape's edge a dark tone of what it encloses, not a uniform black line.

THE FLINCH: a sharp, readable reaction to a blow, in the spirit of a Doom pain frame. It plays for a third of a second when she is struck by a sword or a fire bolt. It must read clearly as hurt, not as an attack.
- Cell 2, JOLT: the instant of the hit. Her head snapped back, the hood falling back from her face, her mouth open in a shriek, the free arm flung up, a few icicles of the mantle snapping off as solid pale pieces right beside her; she clutches the staff to keep her feet.
- Cell 3, STAGGER: a moment later. She doubles forward over the staff, lower than standing, the free hand clutching her middle, the head bowed, the lantern's light dimmed.
