Pixel-art sprite sheet: the WALK cycle of the OGRE, a hulking brute with a two-handed stone maul, for a Doom/Hexen-style first-person game where monsters are drawn as sprites from several camera angles.

REFERENCE IMAGES, in the order attached:
1. The ogre's turnaround sheet (front, front three-quarter, side, rear three-quarter, back). It is the character design: a huge, hulking brute, hunched forward, much broader than a man, with massive rounded shoulders, a barrel chest, a heavy gut, arms thick as tree trunks hanging to its knees and short bowed legs; warty grey-green hide, mottled darker on the back, with old red scars; a small head set low between the shoulders, lank black hair, a heavy underbite jaw with two thick yellow tusks, small yellow eyes; riveted iron bands round both wrists and the upper left arm, a chain with an iron ring hanging across the chest; a ragged brown hide loincloth on a belt, hide strips wound round the shins; and a two-handed STONE MAUL, a grey stone block bound with iron cross-bands on a thick wooden haft ringed with iron, held in its RIGHT hand. Also the shading style.
2. The ogre standing, seen from THIS sheet's camera view. Match its camera angle, the way it faces, its pixel size, outline weight, scale and ground line exactly.

The maul is held by its RIGHT hand in every frame (when both hands hold it, the right hand is the one nearer the stone head); the iron band on the upper LEFT arm stays on the left. Never swap them, never draw a second maul or a third arm. The head, tusks, chain, bands and loincloth look the same in every frame, and the ogre stays exactly as big and as broad as in reference 2.

LAYOUT
- One horizontal row of 5 equal cells, read left to right, each cell as wide as reference 2: cell 1 the standing copy, cells 2 to 5 the 4 frames.
- Background: flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) everywhere, with no gradient, texture, vignette, glow, cast shadow or floor.
- The cells are separated by thin vertical lines of pure cyan (#00FFFF), each running the full height of the image.
- Cell 1 is a plain copy of reference 2: the ogre standing, exactly as tall, in exactly the same pose. It sets the scale for the other frames, which are drawn at the same scale.
- The feet stand on the same ground line in every cell, at the same height as in reference 2. It does not hop or float.
- The whole figure, including the maul when it is raised overhead, fits inside each cell with a clear magenta margin. Nothing touches a cell edge or a cyan line, and nothing is cropped.
- No text, labels, numbers, frame borders, speed lines, motion blur, smear trails, sparks, dust, debris, cracks in the floor, blood or impact effects. Only the ogre and its maul.
- Crisp pixel art with hard edges and no anti-aliasing against the magenta. No semi-transparent pixels, and no pink or purple glow bleeding into the background.

THE WALK: a slow, heavy, lumbering stomp, every step shaking the floor, EXAGGERATED like a cartoon brute so that it reads at a small sprite size. Each of the four frames must differ CLEARLY from the standing copy in cell 1 and from the frame before it: never four near-copies of the standing pose. The ogre stays hunched, the head thrust forward and low. The whole body sways far from side to side with each step, the hump of the shoulders tilting down over the leg that carries its weight. The RIGHT hand drags the maul behind it by the end of its haft: the haft slants back and down, and the stone head scrapes along the floor BEHIND the right foot, no longer standing upright beside it as in cell 1. The free LEFT arm swings far forward and back, opposite to the left leg, knuckles near the knee.
- Frame 2 (cell 2), LEFT CONTACT: a long stride, the left foot planted far forward, the right leg stretched far back on its toes; the body dropped to its lowest, the shoulders tilted down to the left over the planted leg; the left arm swung back, the maul dragging back.
- Frame 3 (cell 3), PASSING: the right knee lifted HIGH, up to the height of the loincloth, the right foot clear off the ground swinging forward past the planted left leg; the body standing tall on the straight left leg, at its highest.
- Frame 4 (cell 4), RIGHT CONTACT: the mirror of frame 2: the right foot planted far forward, the left leg stretched far back; the body at its lowest, the shoulders tilted down to the right over the planted leg; the left arm swung forward.
- Frame 5 (cell 5), PASSING: the mirror of frame 3: the left knee lifted HIGH, the left foot clear off the ground swinging forward past the planted right leg; the body at its highest.
Seen from the front, a stride shows as the forward foot lower on the image and nearer the viewer, the back foot higher and partly hidden behind the leg, and a lifted knee as a leg bent up high with the foot off the ground; seen from the side, the legs scissor wide apart. The four frames loop: after frame 5 comes frame 2 again.
In EVERY frame the RIGHT fist is closed round the end of the maul's haft, and the haft runs unbroken from that fist down to the stone head on the floor: the ogre never lets go of the maul, and the maul never stands or lies on its own.
