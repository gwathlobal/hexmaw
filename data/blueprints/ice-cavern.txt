; The big glacier cave: a pillar of ice in the snow, a frozen pond with another standing
; in it, an octagonal shelf of ice (slippery, a monster on it) and icicles in the west.
; Its plan is drawn by tools/cave_plans.py.
cells 4 3
tags room
weight 0.5
mirror yes
remap wall ice-rock
remap ceiling ice-rock
remap floor snow
legend
  + door ice                                        ; ice caves join only ice caves and gates
  _ pit 0.2, floor ice                              ; the frozen pond, a short step down
  [ diagonal / floor -0.2 0, pit 0.2, floor ice     ; its shore: snow on one half, ice on the other
  ] diagonal \ floor -0.2 0, pit 0.2, floor ice
  < diagonal /, pit 0.2, floor ice                  ; 45-degree ice-rock standing in the pond
  > diagonal \, pit 0.2, floor ice
  , box 0 0.4 ice                                   ; a shelf of ice, one step up, slippery
  { diagonal / ice 0 0.4                            ; its edge
  } diagonal \ ice 0 0.4
  j box 0 0.4 ice, spawn any north
  v box 3 top ice-rock                              ; icicles, hanging from 4 m to 3 m
  A diagonal / ice-rock 3 top
  B diagonal \ ice-rock 3 top
  x box 3 top ice-rock, pit 0.2, floor ice          ; the same over the pond
  P diagonal / ice-rock 3 top, pit 0.2, floor ice
  Q diagonal \ ice-rock 3 top, pit 0.2, floor ice
  i spawn any south

plan 0
