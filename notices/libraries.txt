Every Lisp library the game is built from, compiled into the executable (into Hexmaw.app's
sbcl.core on macOS). The list is the same for every platform, so a few of them are used only
on Windows, macOS or Linux, and a few only while building. Each entry gives the release in
the pinned library dists, its systems, the licence its own definition states, and the
licence text below that is its own. For the few releases that have no licence file, the
text is their licence's standard one under the copyright their sources state.
