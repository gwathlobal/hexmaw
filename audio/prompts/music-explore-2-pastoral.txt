Melodic dungeon exploration music for a 1990s first-person fantasy game, for crypts and catacombs. Moderately slow, around 80 BPM, in a minor key. A clear, haunting melody is always being played by a solo instrument, passed between a wooden flute and an oboe, over plucked lute and harp arpeggios that never stop, pizzicato cellos walking a bass line, soft string chords that change every bar, and a light hand drum and tambourine. Something is always moving: no drones, no long held notes on their own, no ambient washes or pads. Sounds like a General MIDI orchestral soundtrack played through a 1990s sound card wavetable: slightly synthetic, clean, no modern cinematic bombast. Keeps the same instruments and energy all the way through: no intro, no big build, no ending or fade-out, as it will loop for as long as the player explores.
